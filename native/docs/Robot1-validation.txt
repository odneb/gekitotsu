TESTED NATIVE BUILD — 2026-11-13

Machine: Apple M2 Max, 96 GB; macOS 26.2; UE 5.8.4; Metal SM6.
Game: Development editor binary running -game, 1280x720, 100% render scale.
The original editor was left running; measurements are local samples, not
shipping-build benchmarks or promises for other Macs.

COMBAT CHECKS
60 combinations of aerial normal inputs, both facing directions: pass.
Neutral air beam, forward anti-air, down-air spike, dedicated homing: pass.
No extra meter charge for Assist versions of identical air specials: pass.
Air beam once per jump and reset on landing: pass.
Level-three beam: 7 hits, 400 raw damage, mirrored results equal; Critical 450.
Guard: zero health damage, seven blocks. Opponent behind: zero hits.
Meter cost, recovery and stationary commitment: pass.
All 69 move definitions return to a valid recovered state: pass.
Generated native move table matches its JavaScript source: pass.
Build and whitespace check: pass. Final build: film-build39.
Final clean launch after navigation cleanup: normal exit, HUD-on day/night/storm
captures with screen warnings enabled, followed by CPU combat and forced GC.
These checks do not establish competitive balance. Human matchup testing,
combo optimization, accessibility and controller testing remain necessary.
The broader legacy JS/native oracle still has mismatches, including older
throw/grab differences and the intentionally changed native AI. It is not
reported as passing.

RENDER AND EFFECTS
Actual native viewport renders, HUD enabled, all six lighting presets.
Three-band fighter materials, selective silhouette outlines and hard rim cuts.
Environment light quantization preserves actual cast shadows.
Manual exposure, no depth-of-field blur in the gameplay shot.
Real LIVE-2 simulation, 128x256 at 30 Hz; registered bone/dash/projectile/beam
interactors. Idle and dash render-target readbacks change as expected.
Ground is the actual landscape with a painted albedo; foot contact uses
flattened/rebuilt terrain collision, not an image backdrop.
Unused demo navigation bounds/data were removed after lane editing; the native
fighting AI does not use a navigation mesh.
Authored Niagara dust and rain; rain splashes use the existing collision emitter.
Face/hands super camera, animated 3D beam, ground/air animation timing inspected.
The beam lifetime now follows its active move, including slow frames;
offline capture presentation and Niagara share the fixed simulation clock.
Purchased Male 6 and announcer: 29 imported takes loaded; gameplay events logged
for attacks, specials, hurt, fight, round changes, KO and win.
Recorded audio peaks were checked; full listening/mix approval remains open.

PERFORMANCE SAMPLES
Daylight, cached draw-command workaround enabled: median 16.03 ms;
95th-percentile frame 22.25 ms (301-frame capture).
Storm after reducing rain to 1,800 particles/second maximum: median 16.23 ms;
95th-percentile 23.18 ms (301-frame capture).
Final storm CPU-combat sample: median 16.05 ms, 95th percentile 23.62 ms
(601 frames, with audio, HUD, rain and LIVE-2).
The rejected 12,000-particle CPU rain system exceeded 40 ms in combat.
These are frame times, not a locked-60 claim. Shader warm-up, effects, cleanup
and loading can produce slower frames. Fixed-step video capture is excluded
from performance measurements.

REFERENCE GAP / REMAINING WORK
The six-panel reference is painted concept art.
Contact-sheet order is daylight / moonlight, storm / haze, green / twilight. Current footage still has
simpler robot silhouettes and surface detail, coarser background forms, and
less deliberately composed painted foliage/sky than that illustration.
Mist interaction is implemented but its visual density and motion still need
art-direction review; it is deliberately low and does not obscure the feet.
The supers use available animation assets and authored timing/camera/effects;
they do not have the volume of bespoke animation, facial acting or multi-shot
choreography found in DBFZ or Guilty Gear.
The match HUD was revised; every menu and auxiliary screen has not received
a complete visual redesign.
A packaged Shipping build has not been produced or validated. Existing
landscape cooking issues and unused vendor water-material warnings remain.
See CRASH-NOTES.txt: the reproduced Metal crash has a tested workaround;
other historical editor crash families are not claimed resolved.

CAPTURE INTEGRITY
PNG files come from Unreal's actual game viewport with the HUD on.
Video is assembled from real viewport frames rendered at fixed 1/60-second
world/simulation steps. It is silent; it is not a real-time FPS benchmark.
The separate WAV is the native audio mix from a real-time combat test.
No generated scene image, repaint or web view is substituted for gameplay.
The generated bitmap is only a landscape base-colour asset; its full prompt
and provenance accompany the source asset.
