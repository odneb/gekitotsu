CONTROLS
WASD or arrows: move, jump, crouch; hold away from the opponent to guard.
J / K / L: light / medium / heavy. Space or left Shift: Assist modifier.
I: Special. H: dedicated Homing. U: Parry. O: Impact.
J+K: throw. K+L: quick dash.
L+I: level-one super; away+L+I: level two; down+L+I: level three.
Supers cost one, two or three meter stocks respectively. At 25% health or
less, the level-three super upgrades to its Critical version.
In the air, J/K/L and Assist+J/K/L are the six normal attacks.
Neutral air I: Hot Shot beam, once per jump. Toward+I: anti-air.
Down+air I: downward spike. Homing is H, not neutral air I.
Tab opens the menu. R restarts training; B toggles training hitboxes.
Gamepad bindings remain configurable through the existing menu.

ROBOT 1
Only Robot 1 is selectable; crimson and cobalt are the two player colours.
Other character assets remain available on disk but are not selected/loaded.
Male 6 from the purchased TSUKAERU pack voices Robot 1. The downloaded
announcer covers rounds, fight, KO, time, perfect and win announcements.
The fighter uses curated existing animations with move-specific timing.
Maximum Overkill uses a face/hands charge cut and a continuous animated mesh
beam. Seven hits, 400 raw damage (450 for Critical), stationary commitment,
blockable, long recovery. Hot Shot is 80 raw damage with landing recovery.

LIGHTING
Daylight is the initial round palette; round two shifts toward twilight;
low health and later rounds bring storm lighting. Six explicit preview values:
0 daylight, 1 moonlight, 2 storm, 3 warm haze, 4 toxic green, 5 twilight.
To force a palette, launch the command file from Terminal with -gkpalette=0
(or another value). Omit the argument to use gameplay-driven changes.
LIVE-2 simulates low ground mist, with registered fighter-bone, dash,
projectile and beam interaction meshes. It is not a screen-space gradient.

STATUS
This is a playable, tested development slice. It is not a finished commercial
release, a proven balanced competitive game, or an exact reproduction of the
painted concept. See TESTING-AND-LIMITS.txt for measured results and remaining
visual, stability and packaging limitations.
